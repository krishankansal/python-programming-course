Problem 1: Scholarship Eligibility Checker

Write a program to accept a student's name, roll number, percentage, and attendance percentage. Display the student's details and check whether the student is eligible for a scholarship.

Eligibility Criteria:

Percentage ≥ 80%
Attendance ≥ 75%
Problem 2: Employee Salary Calculator

Write a program to accept an employee's name, basic salary, HRA (%), DA (%), and income tax (%).

Calculate and display:

HRA
DA
Gross Salary
Income Tax
Net Salary

Finally, check whether the employee's net salary is greater than ₹50,000.

Problem 3: Shopping Bill Generator

Write a program to accept:

Customer Name
Product Name
Product Price
Quantity
GST Percentage
Discount Percentage

Calculate and display:

Total Price
Discount Amount
GST Amount
Final Bill Amount

Finally, check whether the final bill amount is greater than ₹10,000.

Problem 4: Student Result Analyzer

Write a program to accept a student's name and marks in five subjects.

Calculate and display:

Total Marks
Percentage
Average Marks

Finally, check whether:

The student has passed (percentage ≥ 40%)
The student has secured distinction (percentage ≥ 75%)
Problem 5: BMI and Health Eligibility Checker

Name
Age
Weight (kg)
Height (m)

Age ≥ 18 years
